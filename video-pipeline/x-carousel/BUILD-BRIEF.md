## Hard Do / Don't

### Do
- Use the **real Clicks logo**
- Use **clicksprotocol.xyz** as the only public URL
- Keep the design dark, sharp, premium, and technically credible
- Show actual product states: inflows, routing, liquidity, reputation, attestation, TES
- Treat **x402 / ACP as payment rails** and **Clicks as treasury + trust + benchmark layer**

### Don't
- No fake domains like `clicks.fyi`
- No invented placeholder logos or generic green "C" marks
- No generic hacker-terminal cosplay as the whole brand
- No overpacked dashboards with tiny unreadable widgets
- No scene that is only mood and does not advance the narrative
